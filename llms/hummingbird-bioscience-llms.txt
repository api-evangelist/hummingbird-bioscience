# Hummingbird Bioscience

> Clinical-stage precision biotherapeutics company (The Galen, Singapore Science Park, with US
> operations) discovering and developing antibody-based medicines — including next-generation
> antibody-drug conjugates — for hard-to-treat cancers and autoimmune disease. Hummingbird
> Bioscience sells drug programs and partnerships, not software: as of 2026-08-22 it publishes
> **no API, no SDK, no developer portal and no machine-readable contract of any kind**. Agents
> should not expect a programmable surface here.

## About

- [Website](https://hummingbirdbioscience.com/): Corporate site (WordPress). Note: the origin
  answers automated clients with a SiteGround "Robot Challenge Screen" (HTTP 202,
  `sg-captcha: challenge`), so machine reads of any page on this host require solving a
  JavaScript proof-of-work challenge.
- [About Us](https://hummingbirdbioscience.com/about-us/): Company, leadership, locations.
- [Science](https://hummingbirdbioscience.com/science/): Rational Antibody Discovery (RAD)
  platform — computational target and epitope modelling, proprietary immunization strategies,
  Abvantage mice.
- [Antibody Platform](https://hummingbirdbioscience.com/antibody-platform/)
- [ADC Platform](https://hummingbirdbioscience.com/adc-platform/): Linker-payload chemistry.
- [Pipeline](https://hummingbirdbioscience.com/pipeline/): HMBD-001 (anti-HER3 mAb), HMBD-501
  (HER3-targeted ADC; first patient dosed January 2026). HMBD-002 (anti-VISTA) was licensed to
  Percheron Therapeutics in 2025.
- [Publications](https://hummingbirdbioscience.com/publications/)
